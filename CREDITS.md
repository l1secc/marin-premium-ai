# Credits & Artwork

Marin Premium AI is the **license-free sibling** of `marin-premium`: colour
palettes, config files and tooling are the same original work (MIT). The only
difference is the wallpapers.

## The wallpapers are AI-generated

All 10 wallpapers in `backgrounds/` were generated with a text-to-image model
([Pollinations](https://pollinations.ai), `sana`) from descriptive prompts, then
cropped to 16:9 and stripped of the generator watermark. There is **no human
author**, so the image files themselves carry no copyright and are free to use,
copy and redistribute.

- Prompts: `tools/photos.json` and the build notes in `README.md`.
- No bundled artwork from any artist, studio or stock site.
- No attribution required for the images.

## Character notice

`My Dress-Up Darling` and Marin Kitagawa are © **Shinichi Fukuda /
CloverWorks**. These images are **not** official art and do not depict the
character under licence — they are original AI renders in a similar aesthetic.
Only the image files are unrestricted; the character remains the property of its
rights holders. If you need a version with no character reference at all, drop
your own images into `backgrounds/` and re-run `tools/marin_generate.py`.

Rights-holder removal requests: open an issue and the offending render is
removed.
